FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the API work?

You send a JSON payload describing your video edit, and our API processes it on our cloud infrastructure. The rendered video is returned as a download URL or webhook notification.

What languages are supported?

We provide official SDKs for Node.js, Python, PHP, and more. You can also use our REST API directly with any HTTP client.

How long does rendering take?

Rendering time depends on video length and complexity. Most short videos (under 1 minute) complete in under 60 seconds.

Can I customize the video editor?

Yes! Our white-label editor can be fully customized with your branding, colors, and features to match your application.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, all plans come with a free tier to get started. The Professional plan includes a 14-day free trial with full access to all features.
